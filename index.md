# Roadchat Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [10-12-2026]_

Roadchat is a Windows desktop chat application published by Aquablox.

## Information we collect
- **Account information**: when you sign in, we collect your email address and a verification code to authenticate you. Anonymous use is possible without an account.
- **Profile information**: your chosen username, username color, optional profile picture, and optional social links you choose to add.
- **Chat messages**: messages you send in each of the public rooms and in private chats are relayed through our server to other participants in that room.
- **Presence**: whether you are online in a room, so other participants can see who is present.
## Screen capture
Roadchat can share your screen or a window with participants of a private chat. Capture only starts when you explicitly choose to start it, you select what is shared, and you can stop it at any time. Captured content is streamed only to participants in that private chat and is not stored by us.

## How we use information
We use this information solely to operate the app: authenticate you, display your profile to other users, deliver messages, and show who is present in a room. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

## Data storage and retention
Profile settings and saved chats are stored locally on your device. Messages are relayed in real time and are not stored on our servers beyond what is needed to deliver them. Account information is retained until you delete your account.

## Third parties
Roadchat uses Microsoft Azure to host its real-time messaging service. No data is shared with other third parties.

## Children
Roadchat is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the latest version will always be available at this page.
